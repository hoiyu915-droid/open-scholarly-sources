SCOAP³

SCOAP³ 高能物理開放聯盟

CERN and the SCOAP³ consortium

Consortium-funded OA programme and repository for eligible high-energy-physics articles. Repository articles are openly accessible; journal coverage is contract- and article-dependent.

由 CERN 主導的聯盟資助 OA 計畫與典藏庫；僅符合計畫資格的文章可繼承 OA 身分。
