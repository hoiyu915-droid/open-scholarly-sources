Jxiv 日本 JST 預印本

Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST)

JST-operated Japanese preprint server accepting all research fields in Japanese or English. Records receive DOIs, are screened for format/ethics/legal/plagiarism issues, can be revised, and remain permanently accessible.

JST 經營的日英雙語全學科預印本伺服器；有 DOI、基本倫理與格式篩查，可修訂且所有版本永久保留。
