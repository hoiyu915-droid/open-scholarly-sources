Research Square

Multidisciplinary preprint platform. Direct submissions are prescreened before public posting; posted preprints receive a DOI and are intended to remain public.

跨學科預印本平台；公開前由編務團隊預篩，刊出後取得 DOI 並作為永久公開研究紀錄。
