Zenodo

Zenodo 通用研究典藏庫

CERN / OpenAIRE

General-purpose research repository supporting preprints among many research-output types. Every upload receives a DOI, but records can be open, restricted or closed.

CERN／OpenAIRE 的通用研究典藏，可存放預印本並取得 DOI；但紀錄可設為公開、限制或關閉，需逐筆判定。
