Sciencepaper Online

中國科技論文在線

Center for Scientific Research and Development in Higher Education Institutes, Ministry of Education of China

Chinese multidisciplinary research-manuscript repository established for rapid scholarly sharing. DOAPR records free access, versioning, moderation and optional review services.

中國跨學科研究稿件與預印本平台，可快速公開全文並支援版本更新；平台另有可選審查，需逐筆確認是否已審查。
