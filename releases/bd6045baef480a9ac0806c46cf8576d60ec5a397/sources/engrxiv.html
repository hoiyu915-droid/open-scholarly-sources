engrXiv — Engineering Archive

engrXiv 工程預印本典藏

Open Engineering Inc.

Nonprofit engineering e-print server for working papers, preprints and related research outputs. Moderation controls posting but acceptance is not a statement of research quality; journal DOI links can connect later versions.

非營利工程 e-print 伺服器，收工作論文、預印本與相關研究輸出；moderation 不代表研究品質背書。
